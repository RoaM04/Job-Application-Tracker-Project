# My Journey Building a Job Application Tracker MCP

## Introduction

My journey with NextFlows Academy started with learning about the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and understanding how it can connect AI assistants with tools and data. At the beginning, MCP was a completely new concept for me, but the training was well organized and easy to follow. There was a website that clearly explained the required work and learning materials, and the supervisors also explained the tasks and requirements. This helped us understand what we needed to do and allowed us to move through the training step by step.

## Choosing the Project

When we started thinking about our project, we wanted to build something that could be useful in our real lives. We are close to graduation, and we know that we will soon start applying for jobs. We thought that managing multiple job applications could become confusing, especially when trying to remember the company, position, application status, and other details.

That is how we came up with the idea of a **Job Application Tracker**. We wanted to create something that could help organize job applications and make managing them easier instead of keeping track of everything manually.

## Understanding the Requirements and Designing the Solution

After choosing the idea, we started by understanding the project requirements and thinking about how MCP could help us solve the problem. We worked with TypeScript and Zod and learned how to define MCP tools and validate their inputs.

As we developed the project, we realized that it was more than simply storing job application information. Our goal was to allow an AI assistant to interact with our job application data through MCP tools. This helped me understand the practical connection between AI, tools, and structured data.

## My Contribution to the Project

During the project, I contributed to several parts of the MCP server. I worked on the `update_application_status` tool, which allows the status of a job application to be updated, for example, from Applied to Interview.

I also worked on listing job applications and searching applications by a specific date range. Working on these features helped me understand how MCP tools communicate with stored data and how to make their interactions clear and reliable.

These tasks also gave me practical experience with input validation, data handling, testing different scenarios, and making sure that the tools behaved as expected.

## Challenges Along the Way

One of the biggest challenges for me was connecting the MCP server with Claude Desktop. Understanding how the local server, configuration, Node.js environment, and Claude work together required troubleshooting and several attempts.

Although it was challenging at first, solving this problem became one of the most useful parts of the project. Once the connection worked, I had a much clearer understanding of how MCP allows Claude to use tools and interact with our project data.

This experience also taught me that debugging is an important part of development. Sometimes the solution is not immediately obvious, but continuing to investigate the problem and trying different approaches can eventually lead to a solution.

## What I Learned

Throughout the training, I learned many technical and soft skills. One of the most important things I learned was how an AI assistant can interact with data through MCP tools.

Before this project, I had not really thought about how I could give an AI access to structured data and allow it to update or manage that data through defined tools. Building the project helped me understand this concept in a practical way.

I also gained experience with MCP, TypeScript, Zod, testing, Git and GitHub, debugging, documentation, and working with project requirements.

In addition to the technical skills, I improved my communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and ability to work through challenges. I learned that when something does not work immediately, it is important to keep investigating and not give up.

## Teamwork

Working as a team was an important part of the project. We divided the work between the team members and worked together to complete the different parts of the project.

We communicated about our progress, helped each other when we faced problems, and made sure that our different contributions worked together properly. Having a clear division of tasks made the development process more organized, while working together allowed us to learn from each other and solve problems more effectively.

## Testing and Finalizing the Project

As we moved toward the final stage, we focused on testing, documentation, and making sure that the project was ready to be used.

We tested the different tools, handled validation and error cases, worked on security and reliability, and documented the project. We also prepared the project for a fresh-clone test and finalized the repository and release.

This stage taught me that building a project is not only about writing code. Testing, documentation, security, and making the project understandable and reliable are also important parts of software development.

## Demo Day

Demo Day was one of my favorite moments during the training. It was exciting to finally present something that we had been working on for six weeks.

During the demo, the supervisors gave us prompts and tested the project by using them with Claude and our MCP tools. We watched how the system responded and how the tools worked with the job application data. In the end, we received a **PASS**.

It was a very nice feeling because the result represented all the work, learning, testing, and problem-solving we had done throughout the training. Seeing our project work successfully during the final demo made the whole journey feel worth it.

## Conclusion

Looking back, this project was a valuable learning experience for me. I started the training with a new concept, learned about MCP step by step, worked with my team to turn an idea into a real project, faced technical challenges, tested and improved the system, and finally presented it successfully.

The **Job Application Tracker MCP** helped me understand the practical value of MCP and showed me how AI can interact with structured data through tools.

More importantly, this experience gave me more confidence in learning new technologies, solving problems, and working as part of a team. I am happy with what we built and grateful for everything I learned during this journey.
